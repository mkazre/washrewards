Product & business model

WashRewards SA is a two-sided marketplace connecting car owners with car-wash partners, with a built-in loyalty programme. Each car-wash business is a tenant; tenants are either fixed garages or mobile car washes. Payments are collected by the platform and settled to tenants.

Multi-tenancy & business models

Every car-wash business is a tenant. The platform uses shared-database, row-level multi-tenancy: a single database where every tenant-owned record carries a tenant_id, automatically scoped by a Laravel global scope and a tenant-resolution middleware.

This is the right model for a discovery marketplace â€” consumers query across all tenants to find nearby washes, while each business sees only its own bookings, services, reviews and payouts. Database-per-tenant isolation was considered and rejected as unnecessary overhead.

i
Access model. A User can be a consumer and/or belong to a tenant with a partner role (owner / staff), enforced with Laravel Sanctum tokens plus policy/gate authorization. The same person shops and runs their business from one React Native app.

Two tenant types

Distinguished by a type field. Both share core entities (packages, bookings, reviews, promotions, payouts); the type drives the booking UI, pricing, and geolocation logic.

Payments & marketplace settlement

The platform operates a collect-and-settle model â€” funds flow through the platform so it can retain commission and fund loyalty vouchers.

  1. The customer pays in-app through a South African payment gateway (PayFast Â· Peach Â· Paystack Â· Yoco Â· Ozow instant-EFT). Funds are collected into the platform merchant account.
  2. Each successful booking is recorded in a ledger and split into partner earnings, platform commission, and a voucher contribution. New tables: transactions, settlements / payouts.
  3. Laravel queue jobs + scheduler compute settlements and trigger scheduled per-tenant payouts to each garage / mobile washer. Mobile payouts include any travel fee collected.
  4. PCI-DSS stays entirely with the gateway (SAQ-A scope). The app and AWS store only transaction references and metadata â€” never raw card data.

Security & compliance

  • HTTPS/TLS everywhere â€” ACM certificates on ALB & CloudFront.
  • Secrets in AWS Secrets Manager â€” DB, gateway & API keys; never in code.
  • AWS WAF in front of ALB and CloudFront (OWASP rules, rate limiting).
  • Encryption at rest â€” RDS, S3 & ElastiCache encrypted via KMS.
  • Private subnets for compute and data; only the ALB is public.
  • POPIA compliance â€” data hosted in af-south-1 (Cape Town) for residency & low latency. Fall back to eu-west-1 for any service unavailable there.
  • PCI-DSS â€” card data handled entirely by the gateway (SAQ-A scope).
  • Backups â€” automated RDS snapshots (â‰¥7 days), S3 versioning.
  • GuardDuty + CloudTrail â€” threat detection and audit logging.
